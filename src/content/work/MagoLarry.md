### Project Overview

Larry ([larrymentalista.pages.dev](https://larrymentalista.pages.dev)) is a mentalist, speaker and creator from Buenos Aires who offers shows, conferences, workshops, coaching and his own products. The site is a single page that presents all of it in one place: the show video, his awards, what he offers, a gallery, the Lmental project, his bio and a way to contact him. The client asked to be presented as a *mentalist*, not as "the magician", so the brand and titles follow that.

The foundation is the system from the Kovalink site, which I adapted instead of starting from scratch. I kept the stack and the main technical decisions, dropped the pinned-scroll proposals (there is only one video here) and the invitation section, and rebuilt the identity.

#### Objectives

- **Mentalist, not magician** - Brand, titles and copy position him as a mentalist, as the client requested.
- **One video first** - Lead with the vertical show video, muted on entering the screen, with a sound button.
- **Everything he offers, in one place** - Show, conferences, workshops, coaching and creations, using only the client's own texts.
- **Two languages** - Rioplatense Spanish at `/` and English at `/en/`, because his professional audience is international.
- **Client-editable content** - Keep all copy in one data file.

#### Project Workflow

- **Foundation and identity** - Reused the Kovalink base (Astro 5, Tailwind v4, Lenis, Resend) and rebuilt the look: a near-black base with a violet tint, a single lilac accent (`#b9a2ff`) taken from the violet of the client's Lmental project and the labels in his video, and warm paper for text and the solid button. Oswald, a condensed face close to theatre posters, replaced Montserrat for headlines.
- **Provisional logo** - Built an interim monogram as an Astro component (a block with a cut-out L and a dot) in two variants, full and compact, and a script that renders the favicons and the social card from it.
- **Show video** - A single vertical video with `preload="none"` and a poster, muted when it enters the screen, plus a sound button that restarts it the first time it is used.
- **Range requests for video** - Cloudflare Pages returns the whole static file even when a range is requested, and Safari on iPhone will not play video without a `206` response. I added a `/media/[name]` route that reads the file from the assets store and returns the requested range, with the total size generated by a script before each build.
- **Sections** - Hero with the client's figures, career and awards, offers, a mosaic gallery on desktop that turns into a carousel with a lightbox on mobile, Lmental and a bio.
- **Bilingual content** - All copy lives in `content.es` and `content.en` in a single typed file. The English object takes the Spanish type, so a missing key fails to compile. Spanish is the source and English is translated from it.
- **Contact** - WhatsApp, email, Instagram and a form handled by a single SSR route. Anti-spam is a honeypot, a timestamp, a same-origin check and shared length limits. The form sends the visitor's language, and the email to Larry says when someone wrote from `/en/`.
- **Consent-gated analytics** - A queue behind a consent banner, as in the Kovalink site.
- **Cloudflare deployment** - Moved the deploy to Cloudflare Pages with `nodejs_compat` (the worker does not build without it because of `sharp`) and documented it.

#### Results and Impact

- **One source for copy** - Spanish and English text, navigation and contact details live in `src/data/site.ts`, shared by both language routes through a single `Home` component.
- **Only one dynamic route besides media** - Everything is static except `/api/contact` and the video range endpoint.
- **Video that plays on iPhone** - The range endpoint makes the show video work on Safari for iOS despite the platform limitation.
- **A guard against publishing internal files** - A `check:rutas` script fails the check if anything internal reaches the build output, a lesson carried over from Kovalink.
- **Accessibility and layout** - Viewport units use `svh`, safe-area insets are respected and the hero stacks below 1024px.
- **No invented content** - The "+25 years, +50 countries, +150 cities" figures and the awards come from the client's own document. There are no testimonials or client logos because there was no material for them.
- **Pending items documented** - The final domain, a definitive logo, Resend and GA4 keys, photos of the creations and the legal review of the privacy page are listed as open items.
